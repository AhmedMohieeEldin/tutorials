# Noah short: video prompts for Gemini (Veo)

8 scenes, about 8 seconds each, about 64 seconds in total. Paste each prompt into Gemini on its own.

The prophet Nuh (peace be upon him) is never shown. He is represented only as a glowing orb of light.
The prompts never use the name "Noah", and every prompt explicitly forbids humans.
If the generated Arabic narration sounds wrong, delete the narration sentence from the prompt; the voice can be added afterwards.

## 1. The beginning and the Light
3D animated feature film style like Pixar, soft cinematic lighting, warm pastel colors, toddler-friendly. A peaceful ancient green valley at sunrise with small mud-brick houses and palm trees. A soft floating golden-white glowing orb of light, about the height of an adult, hovers calmly in the middle of the valley, gently pulsing with warm rays and tiny sparkles. Slow gentle camera push-in. IMPORTANT: no humans at all, no people, no faces, no hands, no silhouettes; the glowing light is the only presence. No text, no logos. Audio: soft gentle music-box melody; a warm calm male narrator says in Arabic: "منذ زمنٍ بعيد، دعا نبيُّ اللهِ نوحٌ عليه السلام قومَه ليعبدوا اللهَ وحده."

Golden sparkles swirl around the planks. Slow orbiting camera. IMPORTANT: no humans at all, no people, no faces, no hands, no silhouettes; the glowing light is the only presence. No text, no logos. Audio: soft hopeful music, gentle wood-tapping sounds; a warm calm male narrator says in Arabic: "وأمرَهُ اللهُ أن يصنعَ سفينةً كبيرة."
